## aere

Fichier : poduna-tressage-aere-v2.png

### Prompt exact

Create one refined logo-symbol concept for Poduna, a warm international grocery-list application. Use the reference image as inspiration for the clearly recognizable soft grocery basket and simplified interlaced weave, but redraw the geometry according to this variant. This is a clean flat two-color vector-like GRAPHIC, not a basket photograph. Solid forest green #245C46 symbol on a fully OPAQUE uniform ivory #FAF8F3 square background. All negative spaces are ivory. No transparency, no shadows, no glow, no gradients, no texture, no lettering, no mockup, no fruit, no leaf, no checkmark. One centered front-facing basket occupying approximately 58% of canvas width, generous margins. A clearly visible conventional arch handle with a large OPEN IVORY interior, connected to a horizontal basket rim; the body gently tapers towards a rounded base. Woven construction should be obvious but highly simplified, broad smooth bands and a few deliberate over-under interruptions, never dense mesh. Consistent clean curves and crisp edges. VARIANT 1, AIRY WEAVE: lighter and more open design. A wide gently tapered basket body with just two upright green ribbons interlaced with two horizontal green ribbons. Generous ivory openings between bands, subtly rounded ends. The rim and handle have medium weight. Elegant, restrained, open and immediately legible. Weaving should look structural rather than stripes drawn on a solid basket.
