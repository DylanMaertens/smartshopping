Flat vector-like graphic design, not a physical basket photo. Solid uniform deep forest green #245C46 background, single warm ivory #FAF8F3 symbol, crisp smooth edges, no gradients, no shadows, no textures, no 3D, no mockup, no text or lettering, no watermark. One large centered grocery basket seen straight from the front, comfortably surrounded by generous negative space, symbol occupies approximately 55% of canvas width. Clearly recognizable basket, wider open rim, gently tapering body, rounded lower corners, soft substantial strokes and generously open negative space so it reads at app-icon size. No cart wheels, no fruit, no leaves, no bell, no separate checklist or added objects. Variant A — DISTINCTIVE HANDLE: the basket body is a clean unpatterned rounded outline. Give it one distinctive continuous handle attached at the two rim ends: an asymmetrical soft arch with a restrained checkmark-like lift at one shoulder, flowing and structurally plausible. Its silhouette must first read as a basket handle, with only a subtle secondary suggestion of a check mark. The handle is the main identifying feature. No woven grid or weave in the body.
